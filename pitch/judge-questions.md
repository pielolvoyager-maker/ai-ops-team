# 10 likely judge questions

1. **Is this real or just a demo?**
The website is a prototype with fictional data. The system behind it runs daily in a real online education business. The numbers on the page come from one live shift.

2. **Why would a business trust AI with its customers?**
Agents follow written rules, claim each chat so nobody is answered twice, and ask a human manager when unsure. The manager's answer becomes a rule. Humans stay in control of money and exceptions.

3. **What stops the AI from saying something wrong or harmful?**
Narrow roles, approved scripts and tone, and an escalation path. Agents do not make up prices, promises or discounts. Anything outside the rules goes to the manager.

4. **How is this different from a chatbot?**
A chatbot answers questions on one channel. This is a team: it works across Instagram, WhatsApp, the CRM and calendars, books real calls, sends reminders, and follows up on no-shows.

5. **Does it work in Arabic?**
Yes. The demo runs in English and Gulf Arabic. Deeper dialect coverage is a priority with funding.

6. **How do you make money?**
A monthly subscription tiered by lead volume plus a one-time setup fee. Customers are businesses that sell through DMs.

7. **Who is your customer?**
Businesses in the Gulf that get leads from social media and sell by conversation: education, clinics, real estate, agencies, coaching.

8. **What about data privacy?**
Lead data stays inside the customer's own tools. Access is limited per agent, and conversations are logged for review. Full compliance work with UAE rules is part of the plan.

9. **What is your competitive advantage?**
I run this in production every day, so the rules come from real conversations, not theory. The shared claim board and manager-to-rule loop came from real failures.

10. **What will you do with the support or prize money?**
Run paid pilots with 3 to 5 businesses, harden security, deepen Arabic dialects, and build a simple setup flow so a new business can go live in days.

## Prepare an honest answer for
- "You are 18, can you deliver?" Answer: "I have run this live for a real business. I am asking for pilots so I can prove it again."
- "What are your limits?" Answer: "It follows rules well and escalates when unsure. It does not replace a closer on the call."
