# 2-minute live demo script

Open https://pielolvoyager-maker.github.io/ai-ops-team/ on a laptop. Test the Wi-Fi first. Keep a phone with the same link as backup.

## 0:00 to 0:15 - Hook (hero, do not scroll)
Say: "Most businesses lose leads because nobody answers fast enough. I built a team of six AI agents that answers every lead, qualifies them, books the call and sends the reminder. This is a prototype of a system that runs every day in a real business."
Point at the hero card: "One live shift: 537 messages, 195 leads, 118 replies, 7 calls booked."

## 0:15 to 0:35 - Set the scene
Click **Watch the team work**.
Say: "I am now a lead on Instagram. On the right you see the six agents. COLD opens, RESPOND answers, BACKUP catches anyone waiting, WHATSAPP confirms, LABEL counts, CHECKUP books."

## 0:35 to 1:10 - Path 1: book a call
Tap the suggested replies in the chat, one at a time. Say as you tap:
- "The lead answers. Watch RESPOND claim the chat. Only one agent can claim it, so nobody gets two replies."
- "The team asks about goals, then budget. A qualified lead gets a slot."
- "CHECKUP finds a free slot in the closer's calendar and books it. WHATSAPP sends the confirmation."
Point at the counters: "Booked: 1. No human touched this."

## 1:10 to 1:30 - Path 2: tricky question
Click **Reset**, then choose **Ask a tricky question**.
Say: "When an agent is not sure, it does not guess. It asks the manager in a shared chat." Answer as the manager.
Say: "My answer becomes a rule. Every agent follows it from now on. That is how the team gets smarter."

## 1:30 to 1:50 - Scale
Click **Run a full day**.
Say: "Now 50 leads through all six agents at once. Watch the numbers climb: replies, qualified, booked. This is what one person's morning looks like when the team works for you."

## 1:50 to 2:00 - Close
Say: "Humans stay for what humans do best: closing and relationships. The agents handle everything before and after. I am looking for partners and support to take this from one business to many."

## If something breaks
Refresh, press Reset, switch to Arabic with the EN / عربي toggle if the audience is Arabic speaking. Fallback: describe the three paths from the slides.
