# 6-slide pitch outline

## 1. The problem
- Leads go cold in minutes. Most small businesses answer in hours, or never.
- Hiring people to answer, qualify, book and remind is slow, expensive and uneven.
- Result: paid ads and content bring leads, and the business loses them in the inbox.
- Visual: one chat with "Seen 9 hours ago".

## 2. What the AI ops team does
- Six role-based agents inside the tools a business already uses: Instagram, WhatsApp, CRM, calendar, Slack.
- COLD, RESPOND, BACKUP, WHATSAPP, LABEL, CHECKUP.
- A shared claim board: no lead gets two answers.
- When unsure, an agent asks the manager once, and the answer becomes a team rule.
- Visual: the six agents around one lead.

## 3. Live demo
- 2 minutes, see demo-script.md.
- Book a call, tricky question, run a full day.
- Say: "The demo company is fictional. The system behind it runs in a real business."

## 4. Results
- One live shift: 537 messages, 195 leads handled, 118 replied, 7 calls booked.
- Every lead answered, newest first, the oldest never left waiting.
- Reminders 2 hours before each call, follow-up after no-shows.
- Be honest: these are numbers from one shift, not a promise for every business.

## 5. Business model
- Monthly subscription per business, tiered by lead volume (for example a starter, a growth and a scale plan).
- One-time setup fee for connecting channels and writing the company rules.
- Target customers: online education, clinics, real estate, agencies, any business that sells by DM.
- Value line: costs less than one part-time hire, works 24 hours, never forgets a follow-up.

## 6. The ask
- Entry into the program and support to run pilots with 3 to 5 Gulf businesses.
- Introductions to businesses that sell by DM and WhatsApp.
- Funding and cloud credits to harden security and add Arabic dialect depth.
- Close: "Give me one business and one month, and I will show you the booked calls."
