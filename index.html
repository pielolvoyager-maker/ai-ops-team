      How it works

      A team, not a chatbot

      Each agent has one job, the way a real department splits the work. They share one claim board, one team chat and one set of rules.

      COLD

      Opens every conversation

      Sends the first message to every new lead within minutes and re-warms anyone who went quiet.

      RESPOND

      Answers leads, newest first

      Qualifies like a top salesperson: one question at a time, in the lead's own dialect, with a budget check before any booking.

      BACKUP

      Answers the oldest waiting

      Works the queue from the other end, so nobody is left waiting while RESPOND handles the newest replies.

      WHATSAPP

      The only agent on WhatsApp

      Sends booking confirmations, call links and reminders. One channel, one owner, one consistent voice.

      LABEL

      Tags and counts every lead

      Keeps every lead tagged in the CRM and keeps the live numbers honest, so the team always knows where it stands.

      CHECKUP

      Fills the closers' calendars

      Finds free slots, books qualified leads with the right closer, reminds them 2 hours before the call and follows up on no-shows.

      The claim board

      Before an agent answers, it claims the lead. One lead, one owner. If a second agent reaches the same lead, it sees the claim and moves on. A customer never gets two answers.

      1. A lead replies
      2. RESPOND claims the lead
      3. BACKUP sees the claim and skips
      4. One clear answer

      Ask the manager

      When an agent is not sure, it does not guess. It asks the manager in a shared team chat. The manager answers once, and that answer becomes a rule every agent follows from then on.

      1. An agent is unsure
      2. It asks in the team chat
      3. The manager answers once
      4. A rule for all six agents

      Sells like your best setter

      • One question per message, never a wall of text
      • Replies in the lead's own dialect: Gulf or Levantine Arabic, or English
      • A budget check before any booking
      • Always offers the soonest free slot
      • A low-cost first step only when the main program is truly out of reach

      Plugs into the tools you already use

      Safety

      Clear lines it never crosses

      The agents do the repetitive work. People keep every decision that matters.

      Never pays, never signs

      Money and contracts always stay with a person.

      Never invents facts

      No made-up prices, promises or results. If it is not in the playbook, the agent asks the manager.

      Never touches a teammate's leads

      A lead that belongs to another team member is off limits. The claim board makes that automatic.

      Sensitive cases go to a human

      Refunds, complaints and anything personal are handed to a person right away.
